Bengaluru City Traffic Police

Enforces traffic law and manages congestion citywide, structured within the Bengaluru City Police Commissionerate under the Home Department.

Top post
Special Commissioner (Traffic), an ADGP-rank post since Nov 2023
Zones

Accountability chain

Who answers to whom

Traffic Police is a wing of the city police, not a separate department — its top officer answers to the Bengaluru City Police Commissioner, who answers to the Home Department.

Commissioner of Police, Bengaluru City
Overall city police chief; the traffic wing sits within this Commissionerate.

Major Projects

What it's currently building or running

Traffic Police's one major infrastructure programme right now is a city-wide AI signal upgrade.

Not exhaustive — a handful of the largest, currently active, publicly documented programmes as of September 2026. Budgets, timelines and scope shift often; each entry is dated to its source.

  • Bengaluru Adaptive Traffic Control System (BATCS)165 junctions by Jan 2026; scaling to 500+ by 2027
    AI/computer-vision signal timing (built on the CoSiCoSt ATCS platform with Arcadis IBI Group) that adjusts green time to real-time traffic; pilot corridors showed ~33% waiting-time reduction.

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

Bengaluru Traffic Police is one of the few bodies here with a genuinely current, division-by-division public contact list — each Deputy/Assistant Commissioner's office has its own listed address and email, which stays valid across personnel transfers.

Why no names below the top tier: the officers themselves (DCP/ACP) rotate; the office email/address doesn't, which is why this page cites the desk, not the current name. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
