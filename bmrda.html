Bengaluru Metropolitan Region Development Authority (BMRDA)

Major Projects

What it's currently building or running

BMRDA's own projects are planning instruments, not construction — it doesn't build roads or utilities itself; it plans and regulates land use for the region around Bengaluru.

Not exhaustive — a handful of the largest, currently active, publicly documented programmes as of September 2026. Budgets, timelines and scope shift often; each entry is dated to its source.

  • Revised Structure Plan / Regional Master Plan 2031Draft
    Long-term land-use and growth framework for the Bengaluru Metropolitan Region.
  • Five proposed satellite townshipsNelamangala, Kanakapura, Magadi, Anekal, Hoskote
    Planned decentralised townships to absorb growth outside the GBA boundary.
  • Town Planning Schemes (TPS)Pilot/proposed
    A land-readjustment mechanism where landowners contribute land for roads/parks in exchange for serviced plots, as an alternative to outright acquisition.

Note: the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR) — the 288 km ring highway around Bengaluru, targeted to be roughly half-open by Nov 2026 — is an NHAI (national) project, not a BMRDA one; BMRDA's role is land-use planning and TOD coordination around it, not construction. Sources: OpenCity — BMRDA Revised Structure Plan 2031, Swarajya — STRR status
