Bengaluru Metropolitan Region Development Authority (BMRDA)

Plans and coordinates development across the wider Bengaluru Metropolitan Region — the districts and satellite towns ringing the GBA boundary.

Formed
1985 (BMRDA Act)
Reports to
CM's Bengaluru Development portfolio
Coverage
BMR, beyond GBA limits
Accountability chain

Who answers to whom

A smaller, regionally-focused counterpart to BDA — its own reporting chain is thin compared to the city-level bodies.

Where to actually take this

BMRDA is small enough that its head office is the practical point of contact for most queries.

Why no names below the top tier: Planning Members and Town Planners below the Commissioner aren't named on a public page. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
