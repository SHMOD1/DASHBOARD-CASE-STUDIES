Bangalore Development Authority (BDA)

Bengaluru's master-planning and land authority — forms residential layouts, acquires land, and administers the Revised Master Plan, independent of GBA's ward-level civic remit.

Who answers to whom

BDA runs on an 'Executive Council' model — four statutory member-seats sit under the Commissioner, each heading its own directorate.

Major Projects

What it's currently building or running

BDA's current pipeline centres on one very large layout and its master-plan revision — most other work is smaller-scale allotment and TDR administration rather than headline capital projects.

Not exhaustive — a handful of the largest, currently active, publicly documented programmes as of September 2026. Budgets, timelines and scope shift often; each entry is dated to its source.

  • Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Layout (NPKL)4,043 acres, Kengeri, 9 blocks
    BDA's largest active residential layout; recently forced onto RERA registration after infrastructure-delay penalties.
  • NPKL Phase 2 expansion5,755 acres proposed, 17 villages
    Land acquisition drive between Mysuru Road and Magadi Road, beyond NICE Road.
  • Bengaluru Skydeck~250 m proposed height, sited at NPKL
    Proposed observation tower that would be the tallest structure in Bengaluru.
  • Revised Master Plan (RMP) 2031Draft under implementation
    The zoning/land-use framework governing all BDA layout and development approvals.

Sources: Deccan Herald — NPKL expansion, News Karnataka — Skydeck

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

BDA's Executive Council names (Secretary, Engineer Member, Town Planning Member, Finance Member) aren't consistently published as a live roster, but its RTI structure names a Public Information Officer for each functional wing.

Why no names below the top tier: Executive Council seats and wing-level engineers are internal postings without a public, continuously updated staff directory. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
RTI / PIO route

One PIO per wing (Engineering, Town Planning, Finance, Legal, Admin)

  • BDA RTI page — eng.bdabangalore.org/rti.html
  • RTI info via the state RTI portal — bda.karnataka.gov.in/47/rti-info/en
Statewide backstop: most of these services fall under the Karnataka Sakala Services Act, 2011, which sets a maximum turnaround time per service and gives you a formal escalation path if it is missed — sakala.kar.nic.in.
