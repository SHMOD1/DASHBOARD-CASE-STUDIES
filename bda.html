Bangalore Development Authority (BDA)

Bengaluru's master-planning and land authority — forms residential layouts, acquires land, and administers the Revised Master Plan, independent of GBA's ward-level civic remit.

Formed
1976
Reports to

Who answers to whom

BDA runs on an 'Executive Council' model — four statutory member-seats sit under the Commissioner, each heading its own directorate.

Chairman
N. A. Haris (reported)
Political head of the Authority, appointed by the state government.
