# DASHBOARD-CASE-STUDIES

## Editable Timeline Dashboard

`index.html` is a self-contained, single-file recreation of a "scrollytelling" profile
page — a hero photo, a sticky left-hand timeline rail, and full-bleed image/text sections
that fade in and highlight the active year as you scroll. It ships with dummy sample
content so you can reshape it for ambassadors, employees, product histories, or any other
chronological profile.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser — no build step or server required.

**Editing:**
- Click **Edit Mode: On** in the top bar to make headings, paragraphs, and years directly
  editable in place.
- Hover any photo and click **Change Image** to paste a URL or upload a file from your
  device.
- Use **+ Insert entry here** / **Move up** / **Move down** / **Delete** to reshape the
  timeline.
- **Export JSON** downloads your content; **Import JSON** loads it back in (or share it
  with someone else). **Reset Demo** restores the sample content.
- Edits also autosave to the browser's local storage, so a refresh won't lose your work.
- **Toggle Theme** switches between light and dark palettes.
