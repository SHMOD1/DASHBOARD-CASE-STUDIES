Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Ltd. (BMRCL)

Builds and operates Namma Metro, jointly owned by the Government of India and Government of Karnataka — one of the few bodies on this map answerable to two governments equally.

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

BMRCL isn't organised by public-facing geographic zones the way a utility is, so its own grievance system is the direct route for anything from station issues to project queries.

Why no names below the top tier: Deputy Chief Engineers and Chief Project Managers are project-specific internal postings, not published as a standing public directory. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
