Karnataka Lokayukta

Karnataka's independent anti-corruption ombudsman, with jurisdiction over every civic body on this map — deliberately structured outside the executive chain of command.

Jurisdiction
Statewide, incl. all Bengaluru civic bodies
Independence
Not part of the executive
Accountability chain

Who answers to whom

Unlike every other body on this map, the Lokayukta is designed specifically not to answer to the government it investigates — its accountability is constitutional/statutory, not administrative.
