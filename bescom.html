Bangalore Electricity Supply Company Ltd. (BESCOM)

Distributes electricity across Bengaluru Urban/Rural and 8 districts — one of five Karnataka ESCOMs, structured as a company under the Energy Department.

Coverage
Bengaluru Urban/Rural + 8 districts

Who answers to whom

BESCOM is run as a company: a board chaired by the state's top Energy Department bureaucrat, with a strict zone → circle → division → sub-division → section field hierarchy beneath the MD.

Governing board / council

Company board

Per BESCOM's own disclosure

  • Chaired by the Additional Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary, Energy Department
  • 8 directors on the board, including the Managing Director and functional/independent directors
  • Ultimately answerable to the Energy Department, Government of Karnataka
Field structure

Zone → Circle → Division → Sub-division → Section

BESCOM's own published field structure

  • 4 Zones, each headed by a Chief Engineer: Bengaluru Metro Area (North), Bengaluru Metro Area (South), Bengaluru Rural, Chitradurga
  • 9 Circles, each headed by a Superintending Engineer — 2–3 per zone
  • 32 Divisions, each headed by an Executive Engineer — 3–5 per circle
  • 147 Sub-divisions, each headed by an Assistant Executive Engineer
  • 534 Sections, each headed by an Assistant Engineer — the customer-facing front line

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

BESCOM's zone → circle → division → sub-division → section structure maps directly onto BESCOM's own contact directory, so you can look up your area's actual current desk rather than a name that may have rotated.

Why no names below the top tier: Chief Engineers (zone), Superintending Engineers (circle) and Executive Engineers (division) rotate on transfer orders BESCOM doesn't publish as a running public list. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.

Escalation ladder

Section → Sub-division → Division

  • If unresolved within 7 working days at the first level, it escalates to the Divisional Officer (Executive Engineer) by BESCOM's own published process.
Statewide backstop: most of these services fall under the Karnataka Sakala Services Act, 2011, which sets a maximum turnaround time per service and gives you a formal escalation path if it is missed — sakala.kar.nic.in.
