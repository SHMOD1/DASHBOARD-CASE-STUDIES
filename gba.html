Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA)

The apex civic body for Bengaluru since September 2025, replacing the BBMP — coordinating five independent city corporations under a three-tier governance structure.

Who answers to whom

GBA is a coordinating apex body, not a command hierarchy in the usual sense — each city corporation is independently headed and only coordinated, not commanded, by the GBA above it.

Governing board / council

How the GBA itself is constituted

Per the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024

  • Chairperson: Chief Minister of Karnataka
  • Members: every Karnataka minister, plus all MPs, MLAs and MLCs representing Bengaluru constituencies, plus the Mayors of all five city corporations
  • Member Secretary: the GBA's Chief Commissioner
  • An Executive Committee assists with routine business between full sittings of the Authority
Field structure

Five corporations, ten zones

Each corporation is independently headed and only coordinated — not commanded — by GBA. Each splits into 2 zones (10 total), run by a Joint Commissioner (KAS) + Chief Engineer — there is no separate "Zonal Commissioner" rank. Named Joint Commissioners below are only as complete as public reporting allows; where a zone's current officer wasn't independently confirmed, it says so rather than guessing.

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

GBA and its corporations don't publish a maintained public directory of Joint Commissioners, Assistant Executive Engineers or Health Inspectors by zone/ward — but the underlying offices (inherited from BBMP) are contactable directly.

Why no names below the top tier: those postings are Karnataka Administrative Service transfers that rotate every 1-2 years, and no corporation currently publishes a live, ward-by-ward roster. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
