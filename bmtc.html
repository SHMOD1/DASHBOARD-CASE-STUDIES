Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC)

Runs the city's bus network and feeder routes into Namma Metro — a state road transport corporation under the Transport Department.

Major Projects

What it's currently building or running

BMTC's current push is electrification of its fleet plus redeveloping its two busiest terminals on a PPP model.

Not exhaustive — a handful of the largest, currently active, publicly documented programmes as of September 2026. Budgets, timelines and scope shift often; each entry is dated to its source.

  • Electric bus induction~9,000 e-buses planned for BMTC (14,750 combined across Karnataka's RTCs)
    Under PM e-DRIVE and PM-eBus Sewa central schemes plus externally aided projects; BMTC already runs 1,700+ e-buses as of mid-2026.
  • Majestic (Kempegowda) Bus Station redevelopmentPPP model
    Redevelopment of the city's principal bus terminal.
  • K R Puram satellite bus stationPPP, East Bengaluru
    New satellite terminal to decentralise bus operations away from the city centre.

Sources: Sustainable Bus — e-bus fleet, Deccan Herald — Majestic redevelopment

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

BMTC's Divisional Controllers and Depot Managers aren't listed on a maintained public page, but its complaints line and portal route directly to the operations desk handling your issue.

Why no names below the top tier: Divisional Controllers and the 50 Depot Managers are internal postings without a public, continuously updated roster. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
