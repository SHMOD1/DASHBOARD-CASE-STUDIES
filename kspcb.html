Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)

Monitors pollution and issues environmental clearances statewide, headquartered in Bengaluru — a statutory board under the Forest, Ecology & Environment Department.

Formed
1974
Board size
up to 17 members (statutory ceiling)

Accountability chain

Who answers to whom

KSPCB's structure is set directly by the Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 — a Chairman plus a mixed official/expert Board, with a Member Secretary running operations underneath.

  • A Chairman with special knowledge/experience in environmental protection
  • Up to 5 government officials nominated by the State
  • Up to 5 non-official persons nominated by the State
  • A Member Secretary who runs daily operations
Major Projects

What it's currently building or running

KSPCB doesn't run capital construction projects — its "programmes" are monitoring networks and court-mandated remediation efforts it oversees rather than builds.

Not exhaustive — a handful of the largest, currently active, publicly documented programmes as of September 2026. Budgets, timelines and scope shift often; each entry is dated to its source.

  • Vrushabhavathi River rejuvenationNEERI-led, High Court-monitored
    Restoration of one of Bengaluru's most polluted river stretches, under direct judicial oversight.
  • Real-time water quality monitoring network77 real-time + 68 monthly-analysis stations
    Continuous monitoring stations placed along polluted river stretches statewide.
  • National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)₹425 cr received over 2 years; implementation reported as lagging
    Centrally funded air-quality improvement scheme; Bengaluru's own rollout has been publicly criticised as behind other NCAP cities.

Sources: Deccan Herald — Vrushabhavathi, KSPCB — Water Quality Monitoring, Deccan Herald — NCAP lag

Reach The Desk

Where to actually take this

KSPCB has ~44 regional offices, one per district, each with its own Senior Environmental Officer handling local Consent to Establish/Operate applications and complaints.

Why no names below the top tier: Senior Environmental Officers and Environmental Officers rotate across regional offices without a single continuously maintained public roster. These links go straight to the agency's own citizen-facing channel instead of a name that may already be out of date.
Regional office listing

One office per district — check yours before applying for Consent

Statewide backstop: most of these services fall under the Karnataka Sakala Services Act, 2011, which sets a maximum turnaround time per service and gives you a formal escalation path if it is missed — sakala.kar.nic.in.
