Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)

Monitors pollution and issues environmental clearances statewide, headquartered in Bengaluru — a statutory board under the Forest, Ecology & Environment Department.

Formed
1974
Board size
up to 17 members (statutory ceiling)
Reports to
Forest, Ecology & Environment Dept.
Accountability chain

Who answers to whom

KSPCB's structure is set directly by the Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 — a Chairman plus a mixed official/expert Board, with a Member Secretary running operations underneath.
