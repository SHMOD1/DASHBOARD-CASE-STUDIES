Major Projects

What it's currently building or running

DULT's projects are planning/pilot-scale rather than large capital works — its role is coordinating what BMTC, BMRCL, BDA and Traffic Police each build.

Not exhaustive — a handful of the largest, currently active, publicly documented programmes as of September 2026. Budgets, timelines and scope shift often; each entry is dated to its source.

  • Non-Motorised Transport (NMT) facilitiesRT Nagar, RMV II Stage, Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR Layout, Yelahanka
    Footpath, cycling and pedestrian infrastructure pilots across these neighbourhoods.
  • TenderSURE road redesignAward-winning programme, implemented via BBMP/GBA
    Utility-ducted, standardised street redesign; won the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs' 'commendable initiative' award for NMT.
  • Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) PolicyApproved Nov 2022, ongoing implementation
    Joint DULT-BMRCL policy directing higher-density, mixed-use development around metro stations.

Sources: DULT — Non-Motorised Transport, Deccan Herald — TenderSURE award
