Bengaluru Water Supply & Sewerage Board

Bengaluru Water Supply & Sewerage Board (BWSSB)

Supplies Cauvery and groundwater to Bengaluru and treats the city's sewage — a state statutory board reporting to the Urban Development Department.

Formed
1964 (BWSSB Act)
Reports to
Urban Development Dept.
Accountability chain

Who answers to whom

A classic three-wing utility structure: political Chairperson, an appointed Board, and an engineering hierarchy running from Engineer-in-Chief down to the sub-division.

Chairperson
Dr. Manjula N, IAS
Board head; political and administrative face of BWSSB.
Reports to: Urban Development Dept. / State Government
Board of 7 Members
Appointed by the state government per the BWSSB Act, 1964, mixing engineering, commercial and financial expertise.
Reports to: State Government
Engineer-in-Chief
Apex engineering officer, overseeing all three Chief Engineer verticals.
Reports to: Chairperson
Chief Engineers: Cauvery Water Supply · Maintenance (Water Supply & Sewerage) · Projects Wing
Each runs one major engineering vertical.
Reports to: Engineer-in-Chief
Superintending Engineers
Circle-level oversight.
Reports to: Respective Chief Engineer
Executive & Assistant Engineers
Division/sub-division operations & complaints.
Reports to: Superintending Engineers
Governing board / council

The seven-member Board

Per the BWSSB Act, 1964

  • Chairperson, appointed by the State Government
  • Seven Members appointed by the State Government, drawn from engineering, commercial/administrative and accounting/financial backgrounds
  • A Financial Adviser & Secretary sits alongside the Board for financial administration
